What is Disko?

Disko lets you rank every DJ set you've seen. Log a set, say how it was, and a few quick “which was better?” picks place it in your all-time rankings. You can rate venues, add photos and videos, follow friends as plus-ones, start crews, climb the Charts, and discover DJs and shows near you.

Common questions

I didn't get my confirmation or reset email. Check spam or junk, then use “resend the email” on the sign-up screen. Still nothing? Email us.

How do I change my username or profile photo? Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner.

How do I delete a set? On your Rankings, tap the ⋯ next to the set and choose Delete.

How do I delete my account? Tap your profile picture → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it. You can also email us to do it for you.

How do I report a user or a post? Email us with their username and what happened, and we'll look into it.

Is Disko free? Yes. There are no purchases or subscriptions.
