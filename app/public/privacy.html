Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Disko is a social diary for ranking the DJ sets and venues you've seen. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Disko is made by an independent developer; questions go to disko.app.mail@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them) and your username.
  • What you log — artists, venues, dates, your scores and rankings, notes, photos and videos you attach, and the people you tag as being with you.
  • Profile — an optional profile photo and an optional city you choose to type in. We never collect your precise location. If you tap “Use my current location”, your device's location is used once to look up nearby shows and is not stored.
  • Social activity — your plus-ones (friends), crews you start or join, likes and comments you leave on sets, sets you save to Want to see, and tag invites you dismiss.

We don't use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics, and we don't sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in Disko users can see your username, profile photo, the sets you log (including scores, notes, photos, videos and who you tagged), your plus-ones count via the People page, crews you're in, your spins on the Charts, and the likes and comments you leave. Your ratings are also combined with everyone else's into each artist's and venue's Disko score. Your city is stored on your profile and is readable by signed-in users.
  • Only you can see your email address, your Want to see list and your dismissed tag invites. When you save someone's set, that person can see you saved it (it shows in their notifications).
  • Photos and videos you attach are stored at public web addresses so they load quickly; anyone with the exact link can view them.

How we use it

Only to run Disko: signing you in, saving and ranking your sets, showing your activity to other users, notifying people when you like, comment on or save their sets, recommending artists and predicting scores from ratings, showing shows near your city, and sending account emails (sign-up confirmation and password reset). We don't send marketing email.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase — accounts, database and photo storage.
  • Vercel — hosts the app.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers account emails.
  • Spotify and Wikipedia — artist photos, bios and venue photos. Only artist or venue names are sent.
  • Ticketmaster — upcoming shows near you. Only the city (or, if you choose “Use my current location”, approximate coordinates) is sent.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete any set at any time, and you can delete your whole account in the app (tap your profile picture → Delete account). That permanently removes your account, everything you've logged, your photos and videos, your profile photo, your likes, comments and saves, your plus-ones and crew memberships, and any crews you started. You can also email disko.app.mail@gmail.com and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Disko isn't meant for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
